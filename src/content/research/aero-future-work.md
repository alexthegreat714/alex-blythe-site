## Local engineering pilot on GX10

**Planning reference v0.1 · September 28, 2026 · PROPOSED**

Return to Aero's intended local engineering-pilot goal: a roughly 12–14B model
working with a larger reasoning model on CFD/FEA decisions. Luna prepares
traceable source cards, synthetic decision examples, and bounded implementations;
the lead model designs and reviews; deterministic tools verify; human approval
protects consequential changes. Includes the work breakdown, data-quality gates,
first milestone, and a checklist for returning to scope when the project drifts.

## Editable CAD translation

**Research starter v0.1 · September 24, 2026 · NOT_IMPLEMENTED**

Investigate native Creo-to-NX feature reconstruction that preserves supported
sketches, constraints, expressions, and edit behavior—not another STEP converter.
Includes pinned source snapshots of Morphe, SketchBridge, SimpleCADAPI/CADIR,
InventorLoader, and Creopyson, plus known limitations and a bounded build brief.
