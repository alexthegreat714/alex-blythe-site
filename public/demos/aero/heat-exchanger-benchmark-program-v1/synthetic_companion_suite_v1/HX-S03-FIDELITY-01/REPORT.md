# HX-S03 synthetic fidelity companion

The study executes three deterministic fidelity levels on a new PCHE-shaped
surrogate.  The 3-D manifold correction changes pressure loss by
`12.22%`, wall-temperature spread by
`15.57%`, and local stress by
`23.86%` relative to the
1-D screen.  The frozen decision is to escalate to 3-D before using local
temperature or stress for a design choice.  These are synthetic reduced-order
outputs, not publication values or CFD validation.
