# HX-S05 synthetic architecture/material companion

This study builds a transparent trade over three architecture families and
three material families.  It freezes quantitative criteria before ranking and
retains rejected alternatives.  The selected synthetic pair is
`straight + Inconel718` under the declared duty,
pressure-loss, temperature and margin screens.

The coefficients are explicitly synthetic and are not the ranking or result of
Pelanconi et al. or any other publication.  Agreement/disagreement with an
author choice is therefore not scored; this run proves that Aero can preserve
criteria, alternatives and a preliminary disposition without pretending a
trade screen is validation.
