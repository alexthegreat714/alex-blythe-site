# HX-S04 synthetic sensitivity companion

The deterministic sweep contains **81** declared combinations of
channel diameter, plate thickness, fillet radius and thermal gradient.  It
separates pressure stress, thermal stress, pressure drop and a conservative
combined screen.  The predeclared directional trend checks pass, with channel
diameter the dominant pressure-drop driver in this surrogate.

This is a sensitivity-method proof only.  The equations are thin-wall and
laminar screening approximations; no paper sensitivity ranking or experimental
truth was accessed.
