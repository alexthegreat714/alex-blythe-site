# HX-D01 synthetic design-transfer companion

Stage A starts with a broad, generic requirement and an analytical duty screen
of `19750.50 W`; the correct disposition is **CFD not currently justified**
for that first decision.  Stage B adds a local wall-temperature limit and a
tighter pressure-drop limit, making manifold maldistribution and thermal stress
decision-relevant.  Aero therefore escalates to a planned 3-D CFD → thermal
mapping → structural/tolerance path.

The selected architecture and material are synthetic preliminary choices, not
an employer design and not a hidden-answer benchmark.  The important result is
the explicit change in fidelity decision when the requirement changes.
