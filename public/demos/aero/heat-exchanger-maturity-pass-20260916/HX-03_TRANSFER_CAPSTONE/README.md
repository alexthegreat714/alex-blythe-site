# HX-03 transfer capstone

HX-03 is a synthetic, employer-independent high-temperature/high-pressure gas-recuperator problem. It is not Blue Origin hardware or an employer requirement.

Stage A uses nitrogen-like streams at 900 K/2.0 MPa and 350 K/2.0 MPa, 0.050 kg/s per side, a declared 18 kW minimum duty, ≤40 kPa per-stream pressure drop, and a compact 0.35 m × 0.20 m × 0.25 m envelope. The deterministic screen passes duty and pressure-drop targets under declared property/friction assumptions and selects a welded plate-fin/offset-strip-fin concept for further review.

Stage B adds a 2% outlet-uniformity limit, a 950 K local wall-temperature limit, and a compact manifold. Those new requirements—not solver availability—are the reason to consider 3-D CHT, thermal-structural FEA, verified field transfer, and functional tolerance analysis.

The current artifact is a first-order planning screen, not a numerical solve or design release.
