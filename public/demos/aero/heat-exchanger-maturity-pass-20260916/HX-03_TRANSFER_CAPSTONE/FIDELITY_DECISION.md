# HX-03 fidelity decision

Stage A: CFD is not yet justified for architecture down-selection; energy balance, effectiveness/NTU, and a hydraulic screen answer the immediate question at the declared assumption level.

Stage B: CFD/CHT becomes required if the distribution and local-wall requirements remain. FEA becomes required only after temperature-dependent material allowables, expansion data, pressure basis, and restraints are supplied. Coupling and tolerance analysis are invoked only if the functional requirements make their provenance and dimensional effects decision-relevant.
