First-principles comparison

For this fully developed parallel-plate model, Q = W h Ū and Δp = 12 μ L Q / (W h³). The Reynolds number uses hydraulic diameter 2h. The model omits sidewall and entrance losses.

Reference: Richard Fitzpatrick, Flow Between Parallel Plates, University of Texas at Austin. This is an analytical solution comparison, not comparison to a measured device.
