# HX-02 source custody review

**Decision:** `SOURCE_LEGITIMATE_OPEN_ACCESS_BUT_RECONSTRUCTION_INPUTS_INCOMPLETE`

The Kim et al. *Energies* paper is a legitimate open-access, two-stream water plate-and-shell heat-exchanger experiment. It is a credible truth-anchor candidate, but the available publication does not provide enough dimensioned topology and material/measurement definitions for a scored like-for-like 3-D CFD reconstruction. The review therefore stops before any solving-side result can be produced.

## Visible before a run

- circular plate / chevron channel dimensions and reported effective area and hydraulic diameter;
- hot-water plate-side and cold-water shell-side flow arrangement;
- operating envelopes, LMTD/energy-balance definitions, and uncertainty specifications;
- the T1-T20 plate sensor layout.

## Unresolved

The manifold/header/port transition is schematic; pressure-tap stations are not dimensioned; stream-temperature probe and bulk-mixing definitions are incomplete; and plate/shell material properties are absent from the available source tables. These are custody/reconstruction issues, not permission to guess a model.
